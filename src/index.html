About MRNU

Helping You Build More for Less

Maple Ridge New & Used Building Materials is a community-driven supplier committed to providing sustainable, affordable building materials for residential and commercial projects throughout Metro Vancouver. Since opening in October 2019, we've helped homeowners, contractors, designers, and DIYers find quality new and reclaimed materials while reducing waste.

Our expansive property features an ever-changing selection of doors, windows, cabinetry, plumbing fixtures, lighting, hardware, and more. Whether you're tackling a renovation, restoration, or new build, our knowledgeable and friendly team is here to help you find the right materials for your project.
