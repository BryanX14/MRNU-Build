Salvage Services

Residential & Commercial Building Material Salvage

At MRNU, we believe quality building materials deserve a second life. We recover, purchase, and accept donations of reusable materials from residential and commercial renovation and demolition projects throughout the Lower Mainland. By keeping these materials out of the landfill, we help homeowners, contractors, and DIYers save money while reducing waste.

  • On-Site Salvage

    Our experienced salvage team carefully removes reusable cabinets, doors, windows, lighting, fixtures, railings, lumber, and more from homes and commercial buildings before renovation or demolition.

  • Sell or Donate

    Have quality used building materials? We purchase select items and gladly accept donations that meet our current inventory needs, helping keep valuable materials in circulation.

  • Easy Drop-Off Process

    Before bringing materials to our yard, please email us a few photos. This allows our team to review the items and confirm whether we can accept them before you make the trip.

  • Large Renovation or Demolition?

    Planning a full-house renovation or commercial demolition? Contact us before your project begins to see if our salvage team can safely recover reusable materials on-site.

Whether you're replacing a single door or clearing out an entire property, we'd love to hear from you. Email us photos of your materials or give us a call before dropping them off. All materials are inspected upon arrival, and acceptance is based on their condition and our current inventory needs.
