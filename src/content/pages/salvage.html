Salvage Services

Residential & Commercial Building Material Salvage

At MRNU, we believe quality building materials deserve a second life. We recover, purchase, and accept donations of reusable materials from residential and commercial renovation and demolition projects throughout the Lower Mainland. By keeping reusable materials out of landfills, we help homeowners, contractors, and DIYers reduce waste and find affordable materials for future projects.

  • On-Site Salvage

    Our salvage team carefully removes reusable cabinets, doors, windows, lighting, fixtures, railings, lumber, and other materials from residential and commercial properties before renovation or demolition.

  • Sell or Donate

    Have quality used building materials you no longer need? We purchase select items and accept donations that meet our current inventory needs and quality standards.

  • Easy Drop-Off Process

    Before bringing materials to our yard, please email us a few clear photos. This allows our team to review the items and confirm whether we can accept them before you make the trip.

  • Planning a Large Renovation or Demolition?

    Contact us before your project begins to find out whether our salvage team can safely remove reusable building materials from the property.
