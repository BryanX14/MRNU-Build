Service Areas & Rates

We offer dependable delivery for new and used building materials throughout Maple Ridge and the surrounding communities. Whether you've purchased a single door or a full load of supplies, we'll deliver your order safely to your home, business, or job site.

  • Maple Ridge (Local) $100

  • Abbotsford $200

  • Chilliwack $300

  • Coquitlam $200

  • Langley $175

  • Mission $150

  • North Vancouver $300

  • South Surrey $200

  • Surrey $175

  • Vancouver $250

Please note: Delivery rates are subject to 5% GST. Additional charges may apply for deliveries outside our standard service area or for special delivery requirements.

  • First Come, First Served

    Deliveries are scheduled on a first-come, first-served basis and are available Monday through Friday.

  • Driveway Drop-Off

    Orders are delivered to your driveway or another safe, accessible outdoor location for quick and efficient unloading.

  • No Indoor Deliveries

    Our delivery team cannot move materials inside homes, garages, or other buildings. Please plan accordingly before your delivery arrives.

  • Prepare Your Drop-Off Area

    Please ensure your driveway or designated drop-off area is clear and easily accessible so your order can be delivered safely and efficiently.
