About MRNU

Quality Building Materials Since 2019

Since opening in October 2019, Maple Ridge New & Used Building Materials has helped homeowners, contractors, renovators, and DIYers find quality new and reclaimed building materials at affordable prices. As a locally owned and community-focused business, we provide a practical and sustainable alternative for building and renovation projects throughout the Lower Mainland.

Our large property is home to an ever-changing selection of doors, windows, cabinets, plumbing fixtures, lighting, hardware, lumber, antiques, architectural salvage, and more. Whether you're completing a small repair or managing a large renovation, our knowledgeable team is here to help you find materials that suit your project and budget.

Save money, reduce waste, and give quality building materials a second life. The MRNU Team Serving Maple Ridge and the Lower Mainland
